City of Dreams Sri Lanka

City of Dreams Colombo is Sri Lanka’s first luxury integrated resort, officially opened in August 2025, combining world-class hotels, a casino, fine dining, shopping, and entertainment under one roof. It is located along Colombo’s waterfront and is designed to redefine the city as a hub for leisure, business, and tourism.

Facilities

🏨 Hotels & Accommodation Cinnamon Life City of Dreams: 687 rooms with ocean and city views, modern design, and vibrant dining. Nüwa Sri Lanka: 113 ultra-luxury rooms offering personalized service and refined hospitality. Rooms range from Premier Life Cityscape View to Oceanscape Suites, blending elegance with panoramic vistas. 🎰 Casino & Entertainment Operated by Melco Resorts & Entertainment, with a 20-year casino license from the Sri Lankan government. Features international table games and cutting-edge slot machines in a refined setting. Melco Club loyalty program offers VIP privileges, complimentary stays, and exclusive events. 🍴 Dining & Nightlife 17 restaurants and bars curated by globally acclaimed chefs. Cuisine spans Sri Lankan, Asian, European, and global flavors. Highlights include: Yoroko Robatayaki (Japanese fine dining) Indiya (modern Indian cuisine) Staten Bar & Grill (American steakhouse) Cloud Wine (elegant wine bar with ocean views) Dreams & Beats (outdoor bar and infinity pool with nightlife vibes) 🛍 Shopping & Lifestyle Luxury retail promenade with designer brands and Sri Lankan artisanal boutiques. Lifestyle boutiques and premium shopping experiences integrated into the resort. Event spaces for conferences, exhibitions, and cultural festivals, making Colombo a new MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, Exhibitions) destination. 🌱 Sustainability Built by John Keells Holdings, integrating eco-friendly technologies and community initiatives. Commitment to sustainable development ensures long-term positive impact on Colombo’s economy and environment. 📍 Quick Facts Location: Colombo waterfront, Western Province, Sri Lanka. Opening Date: August 1, 2025. Hotels: Cinnamon Life (687 rooms), Nüwa Sri Lanka (113 rooms). Dining: 17 restaurants & bars. Casino: International gaming, Melco-operated. Shopping: Luxury brands + local boutiques.
