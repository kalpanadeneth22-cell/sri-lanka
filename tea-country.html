Endless Tea Country

The rolling tea estates around Nuwara Eliya and Hatton create a carpet of emerald hills. This region is beloved for its cool mountain air, scenic viewpoints, and classic colonial charm.

What to explore

Take a tea factory tour, ride through the hills on the scenic train, and visit the famous tea gardens where fragrant leaves are hand-picked every day.
